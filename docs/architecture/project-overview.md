# Project Overview

<!-- CODEx_START: business_background -->
*Updated: 2026-05-08 12:05*

## Ron Group Business Background

Ron Group provides one-stop restaurant and hospitality solutions for B2B clients.

Services include:

- Restaurant design
- Custom furniture
- Tableware
- Kitchen equipment
- Lighting
- Procurement support
- Project delivery

Ron Group helps restaurant, cafe, bar, hotel, and hospitality project clients source products and complete project-related purchasing decisions.

<!-- CODEx_END: business_background -->

<!-- CODEx_START: project_goal -->
*Updated: 2026-05-08 12:05*

## Main Project Goal

The project goal is to build a local MVP workflow that helps Ron Group:

- Find potential B2B customer companies
- Collect useful customer information
- Enrich contact methods for outreach
- Score and prioritize leads
- Prepare cleaner lists for future cold email follow-up

The main goal is not the dashboard. The dashboard is only a local support tool for reviewing lead quality.

<!-- CODEx_END: project_goal -->

## Current Market Focus

The current project focus is Australia first.

Before expanding to other countries, the workflow should prove that Ron Group can consistently find useful Australia restaurant and hotel final-customer leads with key-person contact paths.

The near-term goal is not broad global coverage. The near-term goal is to win the Australia workflow first, then turn the successful search, scoring, KP enrichment, and reporting pattern into a repeatable local tool.

<!-- CODEx_END: current_market_focus -->

## Goal Levels

Long-term project goal:

- Build a repeatable lead research system for restaurant, hotel, cafe, bar, hospitality project, design, fit-out, procurement, FF&E, OS&E, and commercial kitchen opportunities across multiple markets.
- Keep the workflow local, reviewable, source-based, and suitable for future cold email preparation.

Current Australia-stage goal:

- Focus on Australia restaurant and hotel final customers first.
- Prioritize key-person discovery over raw lead volume or general contact forms.
- Treat a key person with email as the strongest practical outreach target, with phone as an additional strength signal.
- Treat company email, company phone, and contact forms as secondary contact paths.
- Use fit-out, design, procurement, FF&E, OS&E, and commercial kitchen companies as secondary directions unless a task explicitly asks for supplier or partner leads.
- Prove the Australia method with repeatable queries, scoring rules, CSV update rules, and batch reports before broader tool automation.

## Target Customer Types

Current Australia-stage high-priority customer types:

- Chain restaurants
- Restaurant groups
- Multi-location restaurant operators
- Hotel groups
- Hotel F&B teams or hotel dining concepts
- Restaurant owners planning new openings or renovations
- F&B managers
- Operations managers
- Procurement or purchasing managers inside restaurant or hotel groups
- Event venues with F&B spaces

Long-term high-priority or secondary customer types:

- Hospitality project clients
- Restaurant fit-out companies
- Restaurant design companies
- Commercial kitchen project companies
- Hotel buyers and hotel procurement teams
- FF&E and OS&E project buyers

Medium-priority customer types:

- Independent restaurants with professional websites
- Cafes with expansion or renovation potential
- Bars and lounges
- Boutique hotels
- Interior design companies serving hospitality clients
- Event venues with F&B spaces

Low-priority or unsuitable customer types:

- Very small food stalls or temporary vendors
- Businesses without a clear website or identity
- Permanently closed restaurants
- Consumer or home kitchen buyers
- Businesses outside restaurant, hotel, cafe, bar, or hospitality projects
- Direct competitors selling the same core services

## High-Level MVP Scope

The MVP should stay local and simple.

Included:

- Public-source lead discovery
- Contact enrichment
- CSV-based lead storage
- Manual scoring and review
- Read-only local dashboard for reviewing `leads.csv`
- Timestamped backups and update tracking

Not included yet:

- Automatic email sending
- Login system
- Complex database
- Multi-user collaboration
- Paid data enrichment
- Private or login-only data scraping
- Cloud deployment
